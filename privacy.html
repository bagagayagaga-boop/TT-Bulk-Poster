Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy covers "TikTok Scheduler", a personal-use tool operated by Andrew Spowage ("I", "me") to schedule and publish my own videos to my own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

What the app handles

  • TikTok account details: my TikTok open ID, username and display name, and the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when I log in.
  • My video files, thumbnails, captions and posting schedule.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored on my own computer. TikTok tokens are encrypted at rest. Nothing is stored on a public server, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Who receives data

  • TikTok, to log in and publish my videos, under TikTok's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Anthropic, only if I choose to enable automatic captions: a few still frames from a video are sent to Anthropic's API to draft a caption.

Retention and deletion

I can disconnect TikTok in the app at any time, which revokes the tokens and deletes them from my computer. I can also revoke access in TikTok's settings. Deleting the app's data folder removes everything else.
